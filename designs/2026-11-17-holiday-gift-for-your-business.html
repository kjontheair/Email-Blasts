A question for you

You're showing people what your product does. But are you showing them what a gift it is?

Holiday shoppers aren't really shopping for a product. They're shopping for someone they love. They want the thing that makes their sister laugh, gives their mom a calmer morning, or finally keeps the cat off the kitchen counter.

When you show the feeling your product creates, it stops being "a thing" and becomes "the perfect gift."

☕ The 5-Minute Holiday Gift Challenge

Grab a coffee (and a cat, if one is nearby) and answer these:

1
 What problem made you start this in the first place?
Think of the moment you said, "There has to be a better way."
2
 What does life look like once that problem is solved?
Less mess? More sleep? A happier cat?
3
 Who would light up getting that as a gift?
Picture one real person. The brand-new cat mom. The friend who has everything.
4
 What would make a shopper say, "That is SO them"?
One detail, one moment, one feeling.
5
 How can you show it instead of telling it?
A customer's photo, a quick before-and-after, a two-line review, or a 15-second video.

✨ Now turn it into one post

Your one thing to do today: create one holiday post or short video with this formula.

Know someone who [the problem]?
[Your product] [what changes for them].
It's perfect for [who would love it], because [the feeling].
[Your photo, video, or customer quote]
Give it here: [link] 🎁

Here's what that looks like

"Know someone whose cat treats the couch like a scratching post? Our sisal tower gives cats a better place to scratch and gives their people their couch back. Perfect for the new cat parent still mourning their throw pillows. 🛋️🐾 Shop the holiday collection: [link]"

Not selling a product? This works for you too.

🩺 Vets, ERs & pet services

Your gift is peace of mind. Show the moment you make easier, right when people need it most: "Know someone whose cat gets into everything during the holidays? Save our number. We're here [your holiday hours] if tinsel or table scraps turn into an emergency." Offer gift cards? Try: "Know a brand-new cat parent? Give them [your service], the gift of a healthy, happy start."

🩷 Rescues & nonprofits

Your gift is what a donation makes possible. Swap the problem for a cat who needs help, and the transformation for what a gift does: "Know someone who loves cats more than anything? A [amount] gift in their name gives a foster kitten [what it covers]." A donation in someone's honor is one of the most meaningful gifts there is.

That's it. Five minutes, one post, and shoppers can see exactly why you belong on their list.
