Hi friend,

When was the last time you rested without explaining why?

So many of us treat rest like a reward we have to earn. Finish the list, *then* sit down. Answer every email, *then* breathe. But the list never really ends, does it?

Here's what I've learned from years of living with cats: they never apologize for a nap. They find the sunniest spot in the house, curl up, and fully commit. No guilt. No "I really should be doing something."

> **Your permission slip for today:** Pick one 15-minute window and rest on purpose. No phone, no multitasking. Just you and a warm spot.

Rest is one of the six SuPURR pillars for a reason. It's not the opposite of showing up. It's how we keep showing up.
